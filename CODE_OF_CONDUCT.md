# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Forge to be a welcoming, respectful, and useful project for everyone.
Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to participate in
good faith and to help create a harassment-free experience, regardless of
background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, reviews, discussions, and code.
- Assume good intent while staying attentive to impact.
- Welcome different approaches and technical experience levels.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Protect other people's privacy and do not share personal or confidential
  information without permission.
- Follow the project's security guidance. Do not post credentials, private
  vulnerability details, or sensitive repository content publicly; use the
  process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized
language or attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private
information without consent are not acceptable. Threats, unwanted contact, and
other conduct that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe are also not
acceptable.

Disagreement about code, design, or project direction is welcome when it stays
focused on the work and remains respectful.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project
maintainers through GitHub. For conduct that should not be reported publicly,
contact a maintainer privately through GitHub and include the relevant links,
context, and any supporting evidence. Do not investigate, confront, or expose
the reporter publicly.

Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect confidentiality where possible,
and take the action they consider appropriate. Possible actions include a
warning, removal of content, temporary or permanent participation limits, or
removal from the project. Security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
instead.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces, including the repository, issue tracker,
pull requests, discussions, and project-related events or communication.
